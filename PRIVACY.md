# PlayTube Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

PlayTube needs no account and does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Stored only on your device
Watch history, search history, subscriptions, playlists, favorites, watch later, downloads, settings and local crash logs. Crash logs are shared only when you choose to share them. You can erase everything in Settings → Clear data, and uninstalling the app removes it all.

## Network connections the app makes
- YouTube and Google servers (youtube.com, googlevideo.com, ytimg.com) to search, load videos, comments, thumbnails and streams. Like any browser, these servers see your IP address.
- SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app), only when enabled: it receives the first 4 characters of a hash of the video ID, never the full ID or who you are.
- lrclib.net, for lyrics: it receives the song title, channel name and duration.
- The update server (hosted on Cloudflare): the app fetches a small version file when it opens, and downloads the new APK when you update. The server can see your IP address.
- Telegram and GitHub links open in your browser or app only when you tap them.

Voice search uses the speech-recognition service on your phone, which follows its own provider's privacy policy.

PlayTube is not directed at children. This policy changes only with a new app release.
